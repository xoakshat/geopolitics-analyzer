# Key Findings

## Military spending trends (2000–2023)

Saudi Arabia consistently spends the highest percentage of GDP on 
military among the countries analyzed — hovering around 6–7% across 
most of the period. This reflects its regional security posture in 
the Gulf.

India's military spending has remained relatively stable at around 
2.4–2.6% of GDP despite its economy nearly tripling in size. This 
means the absolute amount spent grew enormously, even as the 
percentage stayed flat.

## GDP growth

China's GDP growth from 2000 to 2023 is the most dramatic in this 
dataset — growing from roughly $1.2 trillion to over $17 trillion. 
India shows strong growth in the same period but from a smaller base.

## What I found interesting

The countries that spend the highest percentage of GDP on military 
are not always the richest. Some smaller economies allocate a 
disproportionately large share of their output to defense, which 
raises interesting questions about threat perception and 
political priorities.
